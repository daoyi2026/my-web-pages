# Temple of Heaven — architectural reference audit

Research date: 2026-10-28. Scope: the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests and its immediate setting. This is a source collection and model correction brief, not a completed reconstruction.

## Previous model provenance

The first model used public descriptive text from the Temple of Heaven official website, Visit Beijing, and UNESCO. It did not use architectural drawings or a photographic reference set. Verified counts were mixed with invented radii, roof profiles, vertical spacing, enclosure panels, railings, stairs, and finial geometry. Passing interaction checks did not establish architectural fidelity.

The scan was downloaded and inspected as images. Exports U10–U12 accompany this dossier. Their presence in an official dossier does not establish a contemporary survey date or an open image license.

## Published cutaway and photographic interpretation

Li Qianlang, *The Emperor's Domain — Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests*, hosted by the Palace Museum, *Forbidden City*, 2009 issue 2. Publication index: https://www.dpm.org.cn/onlines_detail/208206.html . Article entry: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/208156.html . Individual page URLs are recorded in palace-sources.json.

- Printed p.007: cutaway showing the square central frame, circular surrounding structure, enclosure and roof organization. An explanatory architectural illustration, not a dimensioned construction drawing. Author is Li Qianlang; do not attribute this drawing to Liang Sicheng.
- Printed p.010: exterior photograph, useful for the overall hall-to-terrace relationship.
- Printed p.011: finial/eaves, column painting and interior photographs. Useful for comparing the real visual hierarchy against a model.
- Printed p.006: explains unequal eave spacing and the enclosure of screen doors/windows at outer column positions. OCR loses decimal points in dimensions; never copy its raw OCR numbers into geometry.

## Required corrections before rebuilding

| Topic | Defect in initial model | Evidence-led correction |
|---|---|---|
| Overall proportions | Hall oversized against the terraces; roof tiers generic | Register silhouette against U11 and check against south-wide photograph |
| Roof hierarchy | Similar generic roof profiles and arbitrary gaps | Match each roof's distinct profile and unequal gaps using U11, p.007 and exterior photos |
| Enclosure | Continuous red polygonal panels | Model screen doors/windows, frame divisions and entrances from photos |
| Upper frame | Roofs appear to rest on plain cylindrical bands | Add the supported eave/frame hierarchy visible in the cutaway; do not assert unverified joint dimensions |
| Brackets and painting | Missing; replaced by plain bands | Build recognizable bracket clusters and painted panel divisions from close-ups |
| Columns | Correct total but arbitrary radii, positions, diameters and height relationships | Keep 4 + 12 + 12 as verified; obtain/read the detailed plan before claiming measured column geometry |
| Caisson | Absent | Include visible circular, layered interior structure supported by cutaway and interior photo |
| Terrace | Generic posts, a single narrow stair; wrong visual ratio | Rebuild three terrace rings, actual stair arrangement and stone railing profiles from U10/U11/photos |
| Finial | Stack of cylinders | Match photographed curved crown, neck and layered base |

## Remaining evidence gaps

- A readable, dimensioned individual hall plan and a detailed frame section.
- Exact column-circle radii, pillar diameters and member elevations tied to a consistent survey datum.
- Bracket-set dimensions, precise bay allocation, hidden roof framing and joints.
- A comprehensive photographic circuit around all facades and detailed stone-carving coverage.
- Clear identification of the intended survey/restoration date for any claim of historical reconstruction.

The material acquired is sufficient to correct the major visible form and design a much richer educational cutaway. It is not sufficient to claim a complete measured restoration. No such claim should accompany the rebuilt artifact.
